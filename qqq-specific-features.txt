## 1. MacroStatusBar (Minervini-style Market Filter)
##    File: src/components/ui/MacroStatusBar.jsx (kept in codebase, unused)
##
##    Purpose:
##      Shows QQQ's position relative to its 50-day and 200-day SMAs.
##      Contextualizes every intraday signal — a bull setup in a bear macro
##      carries extra risk. Based on Mark Minervini's Trend Template.
##
##    Logic:
##      - Fetches 200+ daily bars for QQQ
##      - Computes SMA(50) and SMA(200) of daily close
##      - Bullish:  price > SMA50 > SMA200, both MAs trending up
##      - Neutral:  price between MAs, or MAs flat
##      - Bearish:  price < SMA50, SMA200 trending down or price below it
##
##    Display:
##      - "Bull Trend" pill (green) — QQQ above both MAs, 50MA rising
##      - "Bear Trend" pill (red)   — QQQ below 200MA
##      - "Mixed" pill (yellow)     — everything else
##      - Shows % distance from 50MA and 200MA
##
##    Props received from App.jsx:
##      { price, sma50, sma200 }
##      Computed via useDailyBars() hook → sma(50), sma(200) of daily closes
##
##    Re-integration notes:
##      - Could be made symbol-aware by passing selectedSymbol
##      - useDailyBars already uses selectedSymbol from the store
##      - Main value is QQQ-specific context (academic ORB research, Minervini)
##      - For other symbols, the bull/bear/mixed classification still works
##        but the QQQ-specific commentary and academic backing doesn't apply
